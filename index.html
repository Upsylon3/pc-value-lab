How scoring works

Each PC gets a performance score: CPU, GPU, RAM and storage compared with a top-end yardstick PC, then weighted. Value divides that by price, so a cheaper PC can beat a stronger one. DDR4 is slightly penalised against DDR5. Assumed RAM/SSD values are marked with ~.
